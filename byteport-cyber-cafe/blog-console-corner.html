Console corner is open: PS5 and Switch nights

Couch co-op, split-the-hour pricing and the party games our regulars love.

Sara HaddadArena & events lead · Dec 20, 2025 · 3 min read
Share

The console corner is open: two couches, two 65″ OLED screens, a PS5 and a Switch — and pricing that lets friends split the hour.

What’s in the corner

Each couch seats four with wireless controllers and a soundbar. Games rotate monthly, with the party and sports titles our regulars ask for most.

Split-the-hour pricing

The couch is billed per hour, not per player, so four friends sharing a session pay a quarter each. Current prices are on the rates page.

Weekly party nights

Thursday evenings are party-game nights with small prizes. No booking needed — just turn up and put your name on the board.
