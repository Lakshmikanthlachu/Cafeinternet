Billing FAQ

Good to know

01Is there a minimum charge?
The first 30 minutes are billed as a block; after that we bill every 15 minutes.
02Do packs expire?
20-hour packs are valid for 90 days and can be shared between family members on the same card.
03Which payments do you accept?
Cash, all major cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay and UPI QR codes.
04Is there a student discount?
Yes — show a valid student ID for 15% off station time Monday to Thursday.
