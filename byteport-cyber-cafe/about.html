About Byteport

Your neighbourhood’s glowing login screen

Nine years of printing boarding passes, rescuing deadlines and hosting LAN nights — meet the café and the crew.

0

opened with 12 PCs and one very loud printer

Our story

A corner shop that became the city’s login screen

Byteport started as a tiny internet room next to a bus stop. Today it’s a 40-station café, print lab and gaming arena — still run by the same people who fixed your first paper jam.

  • Staffed help desk every hour we’re open
  • Privacy-first: every PC wipes itself on logout
  • Fair, posted prices — no hidden service fees
What we offer

Mission

Make every digital task — from a visa form to a ranked match — fast, affordable and stress-free.

01 / 03

Vision

A neighbourhood hub where anyone can get online, get printed and get help, no matter their skill level.

02 / 03

Values

Honest pricing, private sessions, patient staff and machines we’d be proud to use ourselves.

History

Nine years, one long session

  1. 2017

    Doors open

    12 PCs, one laser printer and a coffee machine by the bus stop.

  2. 2019

    Print lab launched

    Color, A3 and binding — students started queuing before exams.

  3. 2021

    Fiber upgrade

    Symmetric gigabit and Wi-Fi for every visitor’s own device.

  4. 2023

    Arena opens

    32 gaming rigs, a VR bay and the first Friday ladder.

  5. 2026

    40 stations & pods

    Video call pods and a help desk for online forms and bookings.
