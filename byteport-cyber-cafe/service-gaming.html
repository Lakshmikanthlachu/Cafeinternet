Overview

Our 32-rig arena runs identical tournament-grade PCs so every match is fair. Steam, Epic, Riot and Battle.net launchers are pre-installed with cloud saves, and every station ships with a mechanical keyboard, 8K-polling mouse and noise-cancelling headset.

  • RTX 4070 Ti and up
  • 27″ 240 Hz monitors
  • Mechanical RGB keyboards
  • 7.1 noise-cancelling headsets
  • All major launchers
  • Weekly cups & ladder

Frequently asked

01Can I use my own account?
Yes — log in to Steam, Riot, Epic or Battle.net. We wipe sessions on logout.
02Can kids play?
Players under 16 are welcome until 9 PM with a guardian’s consent form.
03Can I bring my own mouse or keyboard?
Absolutely. USB ports are at the front of every rig.
04Do you host private LANs?
Yes — birthdays, company LANs and school clubs. Contact us for a quote.
